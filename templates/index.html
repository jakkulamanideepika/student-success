About the System

🌲 Random Forest Classification

This system uses Random Forest, a powerful machine learning algorithm that combines multiple decision trees to make accurate predictions. Each tree analyzes student data from different angles, and they "vote" together to reach a final conclusion.

🎯 Risk Categories

🟢 Green (Low Risk): Student is performing well and likely to pass with high marks (75%+)
🟡 Yellow (Medium Risk): Student needs support and guidance (55-75% expected)
🔴 Red (High Risk): Student is at high risk of failing (<55% expected) - immediate intervention needed

📊 Key Factors Analyzed

  • Attendance (40% weight): Most important predictor
  • Academic Scores (30% weight): Assignments & midterms
  • Study Habits (15% weight): Study hours & discipline
  • Previous GPA (15% weight): Historical performance
  • External Factors: Family support, financial stress, health

✨ Benefits

  • 🚨 Early Warning: Identify at-risk students in week 2, not week 10
  • 🎯 Targeted Support: Provide resources to students who need them most
  • 📈 Improved Outcomes: Increase graduation rates and student success
